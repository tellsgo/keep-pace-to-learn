# Post-hoc CIFAR timing budget

This is a time-axis conversion of frozen learning curves, not measured end-to-end latency or a new training run. With Nr=8, selection is the fastest feasible original method to mean 90% at 1/1.82/4.07 m/s:52.661/54.611/54.611 s. At 4.07 m/s the infeasible mechanical ideal reference is faster (50.700 s), so selection is not the fastest when that reference is included.

28 GHz, wavelength .0107068735 m, tau 5 ms, acceleration 400 m/s^2, actuator speed cap 2.5 m/s;390 updates per epoch,40 epochs. Fixed cycle 5 ms, selection 5.001 ms including 1 microsecond switching. Mechanical exact: S=v*tau+v^2/a, T=tau+2v/a+t_return(S), where return time is 2sqrt(S/a) for S<=V^2/a, otherwise S/V+V/a. At 1 and 1.8202 m/s: cycle 18.660254 and 27.30452804 ms. At 4.0686 m/s exact tracking is impossible; only a dashed ideal reference at 5 ms is plotted, with unreachable actual cycle explicitly distinguished from the reference mapping.

Nr=8 final means (fixed/selection/mechanical):92.947/93.923/93.807% at 1;58.813/93.537/93.840% at 1.82;16.067/93.963/93.877% at 4.07. Mechanical times to 90%:196.492/298.165/50.700 s (last ideal only); fixed 62.400 s at 1 and not reached at the other speeds. Selection saves 15.608% against fixed at 1 m/s. Three-seed differences do not establish statistical significance.

Means are pointwise over three seeds; shading is min–max, not confidence intervals. First recorded mean crossing of 80/90% defines target time; no interpolation, smoothing, extrapolation, or averaging individual first-crossing times. Resolution is 390 cycles. Missing curves are distinct from a fully observed curve that never reaches a threshold. Nr=2 fixed/exact1 m/s only have historical rounded endpoints 85.5/93.77%; all Nr=2 curves at 4.07 are missing. Selection Nr=2 at 1/1.82 has 50.710/58.512 s to 90%; mechanical at 1.82 has 298.165 s.

Source 3.5 GHz/40 ms geometry is rescaled by 8 preserving r/lambda. A changed cycle does not rebuild trajectories: transfer of the learning curves to this schedule is an assumption. Computation, evaluation and extra communication cost are omitted; recorded training wall_s is not used. Rail stroke is unspecified.
