# Residual-displacement study: frozen scientific protocol

Condensed English translation of the original pre-training specification. Scientific parameters and decision rules below were fixed before training; this release edit is not a new preregistration.

CDL-A: 23 clusters, 20 rays each; cluster powers normalized; AoA/ZoA spreads 11/3 degrees, AoD/ZoD 5/3 degrees, randomized ray coupling. Four receive and two transmit antennas form half-wavelength ULAs along y, moving along x. Single vertical polarization, narrowband f=0, 3.5 GHz, wavelength 299792458/3.5e9 m, return delay .04 s. Each seed fixes its geometry; independent exchanges redraw phases, with the same phases for forward and return. Residual r=(1-kappa)v*tau. Return phase includes the residual exactly once; kappa=1 gives B=F ray by ray. Backward baseband convention is B Hermitian transpose.

Task: three independent binary inputs, label 1[x0*x1>0], complex 2x3 encoder, tanh(abs(y)^2) and four-dimensional logistic receiver. Train/test sizes 3840/8192, batch 32, 120 updates, final checkpoint only. Adam steps .025/.06, beta .9/.99, epsilon 1e-8, initial encoder norm .4, norm bound 1/sqrt(3); receiver weights .8, bias -.4, clipping 4, L2 .001. Forward/return noise standard deviations .1/.03. Physical return divides by 16 and restores by 16 (noise variance .2304), without calibration or extra CSI. Ideal CSI has 120 updates, exact forward adjoint and no return noise.

Speeds {3,10,30} km/h; main kappa {.5,.6,.7,.8,.85,.9,.95,1}; fixed kappa=0 and CSI controls at each speed. 30 cells x8 seeds=240 training runs. Formal seeds 610150100–610150107; independent precheck seeds 610150200–610150207. SeedSequence/Philox separates geometry, phases, data, noise and initialization. Same seeds are paired across cells; repeated identical CSI/restoration controls do not increase independent sample size. Use full eight-seed means, 20000 paired seed bootstrap resamples, SD, ranges and descriptive 95% intervals; thresholds are not replaced by intervals.

Theory: phi_s(d)=sum_l(P_l/20) exp(i 2 pi q_l,x d), d=r/lambda, averaged over the eight formal geometries. Search d in [0,4] on 40001 points and refine sign changes with brentq; this is not a training-grid extension. Positive lobes use Re(phi), never abs(phi). First three roots: .3517790636126388, .8944161941957102, 1.4442224793679. kappa_star=1-z1*lambda/(v*tau), without clipping. Frozen kappa_star values: .096051056, .728815317, .909605106. K3 pairing is (30,.7) versus (10,.8), abs(phi) difference .013538210238787984.

K1: scan downward from kappa=1 including fixed 0. First measured eight-seed mean below 80% is the operational endpoint; report the adjacent successful point if present, without interpolation or an untested monotonicity assumption. Error at most .1 at all speeds supports the finite-grid prediction; any error greater than .2 falsifies and closes it; intermediate errors are unresolved. No crossing means censoring. Report main-eight-grid censoring separately. The low-speed gap 0–.5 prevents continuous threshold localization.

K2: Spearman rank correlation of Re(phi) and mean accuracy over the 24 moving cells. At least .85 supports, below .6 falsifies, otherwise unresolved. The 27 physical and 30 total cells are sensitivity analyses, not rescue criteria. K3: descriptively match each second-positive-lobe moving cell to a first-lobe cell by nearest abs(phi), ties by speed then kappa; no effect threshold. Fixed second-lobe points are supplementary.

Precheck only at 30 km/h: eight-seed mean CE(fixed)-CE(CSI) at least .10 permits training; otherwise stop. Formal order: speeds 3,10,30, CSI then kappa 1,.95,.9,.85,.8,.7,.6,.5,0. Closed claims remain closed while other prescribed cells complete. No extra seeds, retries, grid extensions or tuning. The original release input lacks this study's original configuration, freeze records, raw worlds and most audit dependencies; see RELEASE_CHECK at repository root.
